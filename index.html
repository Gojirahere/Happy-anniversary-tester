01 / A LETTER

Before I met you

Kammo,

I keep thinking about the version of you that existed before any of this. The one who already had her own quiet world, her own songs, her own places that felt like home, her own way of carrying things without making them heavy.

I never wanted to interrupt that version. I just wanted to be allowed to stand near it. To notice the little rituals, the soft expressions you make when you think no one is looking, the way you still light up talking about things you loved long before me.

You already knew how to be interesting. You already knew how to be soft and stubborn at the same time. You already had a whole personality that didn’t need me to complete it.

And somehow, that made everything better. Because I didn’t fall for a blank page. I fell for someone who already had chapters.

— still reading them

09 / FOR THE HEAVY DAYS

If the day feels heavy

Some days will be louder than others. Some days your mind will try to convince you that you’re too much, or not enough, or that the future is only scary.

On those days I want you to remember this: you don’t have to perform being okay. You don’t have to shrink your dreams because someone else doesn’t understand them. You don’t have to be productive every second to deserve rest.

You are allowed to be curious, ambitious, stubborn, soft, weird, and still figuring things out — all at the same time.

And if you ever forget, I’ll remind you. Quietly. Without making it a big speech. Just the way we already do.

— always on your side
